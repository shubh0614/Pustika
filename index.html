Pustika

Turn PDFs, photos of notes and text files into booklets, pocket books, flash cards, several pages per side, or pages with room for notes, ready for double-sided printing. Everything runs in your browser, so your files never leave your device.

Flash cards

Write cards in a .txt or .md file, with an empty line between cards:

Q: Where do the light reactions happen?
A: In the thylakoid membranes.

Mitochondria :: Makes ATP for the cell

Krebs cycle
---
Happens in the matrix
- makes NADH and FADH2

Water splits at photosystem II
Oxygen is a by-product

Q: and A: make a question card. keyword :: meaning makes a keyword card. A line of --- splits the front from the back. Anything else becomes a note card with no back. Bold, italic and lists work too. Text is sized to fill each card.
